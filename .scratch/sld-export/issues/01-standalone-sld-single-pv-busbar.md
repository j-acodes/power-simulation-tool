# 01: Standalone SLD for a single-busbar PV plant

**What to build:** the tracer bullet. A **Download SLD** button beside the report button
downloads `<design>-sld.pdf`: one A3-landscape sheet for a PV plant with one busbar and an HV
transformer. The sheet draws the grid-side chain (POC → metering → disconnector → HV breaker →
HV transformer → export cable → MV incomer breaker → busbar), the busbar horizontally, and every
circuit as a column of every station in chain order, each station with its load-break switch,
transformer and one inverter symbol. Indicative feeder breakers are drawn. Tags TS1…, C1…, BB1.
Frame, title block (project name, design name, date, "Generated by Power Simulation Tool") and
the "PRELIMINARY — NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION" stamp. Labels are tags only in this ticket; figures
come in 02. Build it as the spec's two layers: a pure `sld_sheets(...)` layout model and a thin
ReportLab `Drawing` renderer, plus `build_sld_pdf(...) -> bytes`, a `POST /api/sld?name=`
endpoint mirroring `POST /api/report`, and the frontend button. See `.scratch/sld-export/spec.md`.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately).
